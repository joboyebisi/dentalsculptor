This study investigates the development of psychological ownership and spatial reasoning skills in dental students utilizing advanced 3D modeling environments (DentalSculptor). By participating, you consent to the collection of anonymized interaction data, including but not limited to: model manipulation metrics, tool selection frequencies, and duration of focused engagement.

Data Confidentiality: All collected data is strictly anonymized and aggregated. No personally identifiable information (PII) will be linked to your interaction metrics in any published research. Your participation is voluntary, and you reserve the right to withdraw from the data collection initiative at any time without penalty or loss of access to the core DentalSculptor platform.

Usage of Findings: The insights derived from this study will contribute to pedagogical frameworks for digital dentistry education. Aggregated findings may be published in peer-reviewed academic journals and presented at educational conferences.

By proceeding, you acknowledge that you have read and understood the terms outlined in this document and agree to participate under these conditions.

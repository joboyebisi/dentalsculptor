Please review the research terms and data governance protocols below.

1. PURPOSE OF DATA COLLECTION

By using DentiResearch Pro, you are participating in a Queen Mary University of London doctoral research project. We track interaction patterns to study pedagogical agency and the efficacy of 3D haptic feedback in dental surgery simulation.

2. SCOPE OF TRACKING

  • Usability metrics and interface dwell times.
  • 3D viewport manipulation and tool precision.
  • Interaction sequences for complex procedural modeling.

3. DATA ANONYMIZATION

All collected data is stripped of PII (Personally Identifiable Information) before being stored in our EPSRC-funded research repository. Your account identity is mapped to a randomized researcher ID.

4. WITHDRAWAL RIGHTS

You may opt-out of data collection at any time via the Settings panel, though some advanced AI-assisted features may be limited as they rely on real-time feedback loops.
